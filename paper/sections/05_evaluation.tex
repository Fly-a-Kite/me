\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\subsection{Experimental setup}

All experiments run on a 80-core, 251\,GB Linux host against a frozen target environment
(pandas 3.0.3, Polars 1.42.1, DuckDB 1.5.4, PyArrow 25.0.0, DataFusion 54.0.0, chDB 4.2.1);
the interpreter binary is frozen by SHA-256 so the environment cannot drift. Campaigns use
excluded smoke/yield blocks and fresh seed pairs starting at \texttt{30700001}. Findings follow
the counting contract finding $\rightarrow$ recheck survivor $\rightarrow$ native candidate
$\rightarrow$ issue-ready root $\rightarrow$ independently confirmed family.

\subsection{RQ0: How often do tabular engines actually diverge?}

We streamed 300 longhaul run logs (66{,}000 executable cases) and counted cases where the
oracle observed at least one cross-backend divergence (Table~\ref{tab:rq0}).

\begin{table}[t]
\caption{RQ0 — raw cross-engine divergence and its adjudication.}
\label{tab:rq0}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
Stage & Cases / findings & Rate \\
\midrule
Executed cases & 66{,}000 & --- \\
Cases with any divergence & 269 & 0.41\,\% \\
\quad of which SQLite harness boundary (NUL literal) & 270 findings & --- \\
\quad of which declared NaN boundary (PyArrow) & 4 findings & --- \\
Genuine engine divergence after boundary adjudication & 4 & 0.006\,\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The raw rate is dominated by a single undeclared harness boundary: 270 of 274 findings are
SQLite rejecting NUL-containing string literals. The same adapter defect surfaced under 17
different \texttt{root\_cause} labels, so root-cause labels are not a valid taxonomy until
boundaries are adjudicated. Declaring the boundary (typed lowering error plus a pre-reference
boundary rule) collapses the rate by roughly $65\times$ on the same workload and backends.

\subsection{RQ2: Does normalization control noise?}

The RQ0 funnel is the direct evidence: raw cross-engine divergence of 0.41\,\% reduces to
0.006\,\% once two declared boundaries are adjudicated, with no loss of the nine confirmed
families. Two boundary classes were found and fixed during this work: special-float filter
literals (NaN-as-value vs NaN-as-null; all capability models already declare
\texttt{nan\_distinct\_from\_null}) and SQLite NUL-containing string literals (typed harness
lowering error). One adapter defect produced 516 candidate artifacts under 17 labels, and
post-fix batches report zero fresh families on the same workload. \todo{Add the paired
\texttt{legacy\_cartesian} vs \texttt{contract\_cartesian} comparison once rerun.}

\subsection{RQ7: Are the confirmed families reachable by the strongest competitors?}

We ran competitor-scope arms over the nine strict confirmed roots: a DataFrame-family plain
differential arm (TDiFf-like scope) and a DuckDB/SQLite SQL-oracle arm (SQLancer-like scope).
Table~\ref{tab:rq7} reports reachability (does the buggy backend lie in the competitor's target
set?) and detection.

\begin{table}[t]
\caption{RQ7 — competitor scope versus our confirmed roots.}
\label{tab:rq7}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
\toprule
Arm & Reachable & Detected & Out of scope \\
\midrule
\texttt{tdiff\_style} (DataFrame + differential) & 2/9 & 1/9 & 7/9 \\
\texttt{sqlancer\_common\_scope} (DuckDB/SQLite) & 1/9 & 0/9 & 8/9 \\
Competitor union & 3/9 & --- & \textbf{6/9} \\
\texttt{ours\_full} (all four families) & 9/9 & 3/9 (differential only) & 0/9 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Six of nine roots are out of reach of every competitor scope; the five DataFusion query-engine
roots and the PyArrow layout root are unreachable by construction. A second finding is that
even our own full-backend arm with \emph{plain differential only} detects just 3/9: the
remaining six require the contract/metamorphic/probe oracle family, not merely backend breadth.
This isolates both the scope claim and the oracle-family claim.
