(15–19 strict confirmed)** with a methodology/measurement primary claim, while continuing the
24 h campaign and upstream confirmation in case the count rises.

## Evidence behind the decision

| Run | Scale | New roots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30-minute engineering longhaul | 5,180 cases | 0 |

Historical rate ≈ 1 root per ~20k executed cases, and the existing profiles are clearly near
saturation for this system. Reaching 20–29 confirmed in 8 weeks is not supported by the data.

## What the paper claims instead (all already evidenced)

1. **RQ7 competitor unreachability** — competitor scopes together reach only 3/9 of our confirmed
   roots; 6/9 are out of reach by construction. (Empirical, committed.)
2. **Bug-evidence audit** — 9 strict confirmed, 4 still affected on latest, 7 severity-weighted
   groups, per-root independence analysis. (Tooling + test, committed.)
3. **Cross-version confirmation** — version-sensitive roots are mechanically re-detected with
   zero false positives on stable roots. (Committed.)
4. **Noise control** — same-system parallel execution produces only expected float
   reduction-order divergence, correctly classified as non-bug. (This run.)
5. **Semantic-contrast methodology** — the HyperContract/observed-coverage/evidence framing
   (`INNOVATION_ANALYSIS.md`, `COMPETITIVE_POSITIONING.md`).
